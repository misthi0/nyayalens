This Service Agreement is made on 12/04/2026 between Verma Pvt Ltd (the First Party) and Sharma Traders (the Second Party).
The consideration is Rs. 5,00,000 payable in three instalments. The term is 24 months.
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
The first party shall indemnify the second party against all losses and claims.
A penalty of Rs. 25,000 shall be payable on breach. The agreement renews automatically unless cancelled.
The second party shall not engage in any competing business for two years (non-compete).
Any dispute shall be resolved by arbitration in Meerut. The courts at Meerut shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
